# Genesis 8 — verse log

One section per verse: bold translation line, pointed Hebrew, transliteration, then a table (Hebrew / Transliteration / Literal / Note). Notes state form and usage only. Rules in method/method.md.

Witnesses for this chapter: MT, SP and LXX throughout; Syriac and Vulgate cited where they differ. No Dead Sea Scroll covers any verse of Genesis 8. One secondary list (the Wikipedia list of Cave 4 manuscripts) gives 4Q1 = 4QGen-Exod-a as containing Gen 8:20–21; the published study of the DJD XII edition consulted on 2026-10-24 (E. Tigchelaar, "4Q1 (4QGen-Exoda): Identification of Fragments and Comments," KU Leuven, citing J. R. Davila, DJD XII, pp. 7–30, pls. I–V) tabulates the scroll's Genesis fragments from frag. 1 line 1 = Gen 22:14 onward (then 27:38–39, 42–43; 34–37; 39; 45; 48–49), and dssenglishbible.com's contents list for 4Q1 likewise begins at 22:14; the "8:20–21" is not in either and is taken as a slip for the scroll's Exodus 8:20–22. DJD XII itself was not opened; 4Q1 stays in the witness table as "extent unconfirmed" and is not cited. The MT is the Leningrad text as archived in sources/wlc-uxlc (UXLC 2.6), accents stripped. The SP was compared verse by verse against the archived consonantal text of MS Dublin Chester Beatty 751 (sources/sp-dt-ucph); von Gall and Tal–Florentin not consulted. The SP differs from the MT in ch. 8 in sense at 8:2 ("was finished" for "was restrained," one letter), 8:3, 5, 7 (finite verbs for the infinitives absolute), 8:9 (a masculine verb for the feminine; "his hand" with the object marker), 8:11 ("leaves of olive" for "leaf of olive"), 8:17 ("and every"; one form for the MT's ketiv and qere), 8:19 (the order of the classes and "the creeper that creeps" for "all that moves"), 8:21 (word order), 8:22 ("by day" for "and day"; no "and" before "cold" and "summer"), and in spelling only at 8:2, 3, 4 (הררט), 5, 12, 13, 19, 20. The LXX is Swete's text (Codex Alexandrinus for Genesis) as archived in sources/lxx-swete-sollupulo and sources/lxx-swete, checked for every verse against the Rahlfs–Hanhart text (2006) opened at die-bibel.de in a browser on 2026-10-24; the two agree except at 8:2 (Rahlfs epekalyphthesan, "were covered over"; Swete's copy apekalyphthesan, "were uncovered"), 8:9 (a synonym for "returned"), 8:13 (Rahlfs adds hen epoiesen, "which he made," after "the roof") and 8:17 (seautou for sou). The LXX differs from the Hebrew in wording at 8:1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 11, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22. Göttingen not opened. The Vulgate is the Clementine text (sources/vulgate-clementine); the Syriac the ETCBC transcription of the Leiden text (sources/peshitta-etcbc), read in transliteration without a lexicon.

The chapter's names for God: Elohim at 8:1 ×2, 15; YHWH at 8:20, 21 ×2. The LXX has ho theos at 8:1 ×2 and 8:20 (where the Hebrew has YHWH) and kyrios ho theos at 8:15, 21 ×2; the Vulgate Deus at 8:1, 15 and Dominus at 8:20, 21; the Syriac Alaha at 8:1, 15 and Marya at 8:20, 21. The chapter's dates: the seventh month, the seventeenth day (8:4); the tenth month, the first day (8:5); the six hundred and first year, the first month, the first day (8:13); the second month, the twenty-seventh day (8:14); and the intervals: a hundred and fifty days (8:3), forty days (8:6), seven days twice (8:10, 12). With 7:11 (the six hundredth year, the second month, the seventeenth day) the flood's dates run from the seventeenth of the second month to the twenty-seventh of the second month of the next year in the MT; in the LXX, which reads the twenty-seventh at 7:11 and 8:4, from the twenty-seventh to the twenty-seventh.

## Genesis 8:22

**Translation: Yet all the days of the earth, seed and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease."**

עֹד כָּל־יְמֵי הָאָרֶץ זֶרַע וְקָצִיר וְקֹר וָחֹם וְקַיִץ וָחֹרֶף וְיוֹם וָלַיְלָה לֹא יִשְׁבֹּתוּ

| עֹד כָּל־יְמֵי הָאָרֶץ | od kol-yemei ha-aretz | yet all the days of the earth | od, "yet, still" (7:4; 8:10, 12, 21 ×2), opening the sentence with no verb: "still, all the days of the earth," an adverb and a time phrase; "while" and "as long as" supply a clause. kol-yemei, "all the days of," the phrase of 3:14, 17 ("all the days of your life"); 5:5–31 ("all the days of X were"), here of the earth: the earth has days, as the fathers had. LXX: pasas tas hemeras tes ges, "all the days of the earth," no "yet"; Vulgate: cunctis diebus terrae; Syriac: u-mekhil kulhon yawmata d-ar'a, "and from now on, all the days of the earth," read in transliteration. KJV: "While the earth remaineth." |
| זֶרַע וְקָצִיר וְקֹר וָחֹם וְקַיִץ וָחֹרֶף וְיוֹם וָלַיְלָה | zera ve-qatzir ve-qor va-chom ve-qayitz va-choref ve-yom va-laylah | seed and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night | Four pairs, eight nouns, each pair joined by vav and each pair to the next by vav: zera, "seed," the word of 1:11, 12, 29; 3:15; 4:25; 7:3, here the sowing season (so Lev 26:5, "the seed shall reach the vintage"); qatzir, "harvest": first use; 54 uses (30:14; 45:6; Ex 23:16; 34:21, 22; Lev 19:9; 23:10, 22; 25:5; Ruth 1:22; 2:21, 23; 1 Sam 6:13; 12:17; 2 Sam 21:9, 10; 23:13; Jer 5:17, 24; 8:20; 50:16; Hos 6:11; Joel 1:11; 3:13; Amos 4:7; Mic 6:15); qor, "cold," one use in the Bible; chom, "heat," about 14 uses (18:1, "the heat of the day"; 1 Sam 11:9, 11; 21:6; 2 Sam 4:5; Job 24:19; 30:30; Isa 4:6; 18:4; 25:5; Jer 17:8; 36:30; 51:39; Neh 7:3); qayitz, "summer, summer fruit," 20 uses (2 Sam 16:1, 2; Ps 32:4; 74:17, "you made summer and winter"; Prov 6:8; 10:5; 26:1; 30:25; Isa 16:9; 28:4; Jer 8:20; 40:10, 12; 48:32; Amos 3:15; 8:1, 2; Mic 7:1; Zech 14:8, "in summer and in winter"); choref, "winter," 7 uses (Job 29:4; Ps 74:17; Prov 20:4; Jer 36:22; Amos 3:15; Zech 14:8); yom va-laylah, "day and night," the two nouns of 1:5, 14, 16, 18 and 7:4, 12. The eight nouns are the seasons' own vocabulary, the words the Concordance's mo'ed entry (row 8) names as Genesis's words for a season of the year. SP: קור וחם קיץ וחרף, without the vav between the pairs, and יומם ולילה, "by day and night," the adverb yomam (Ex 13:21; Num 9:21) for the noun yom. LXX: sperma kai therismos, psychos kai kauma, theros kai ear hemeran kai nykta, "seed and harvest, cold and heat, summer and spring, day and night," ear, "spring," for "winter"; Vulgate: sementis et messis, frigus et aestus, aestas et hiems, nox et dies, "night and day," the last pair reversed. Added to Judging variants. KJV: "seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night." |
| לֹא יִשְׁבֹּתוּ | lo yishbotu | shall not cease | shavat, "cease," the verb of 2:2, 3 ("God ceased on the seventh day"; row 16; Concordance, 71 uses, all stopping), plural, with the eight nouns as subject, negated: the one use in Genesis outside 2:2–3. The MT has a section break (ס) after the verse. LXX: ou katapausousin; Vulgate: non requiescent, "shall not rest," the Latin verb of 8:4 (requievit). KJV: "shall not cease." |
