# Genesis 7 — verse log

One section per verse: bold translation line, pointed Hebrew, transliteration, then a table (Hebrew / Transliteration / Literal / Note). Notes state form and usage only. Rules in method/method.md.

Witnesses for this chapter: MT, SP and LXX throughout; Syriac and Vulgate cited where they differ. No Dead Sea Scroll is listed for any verse of Genesis 7: the published lists consulted on 2026-10-24 (the Wikipedia list of the scrolls, Caves 1–11, Murabba'at and Masada; dssenglishbible.com's Genesis index, whose chapter links run 1–6 and then 8) give 6Q1 as ending at 6:21 and the next Genesis fragment, 4QGen-Exod-a (4Q1), as beginning at 8:20–21 (Davila, DJD XII, pp. 7–30, not consulted). Corrected 2026-10-24 from method/scrolls-genesis.md: 4Q1's transcribed Genesis begins at frg. 1 = 22:14 (DJD XII, pp. 7–30, as reproduced in Ulrich, BQS, p. 9); the "8:20–21" of the lists is a queried entry, "8:20–21?", in the published index of biblical passages (Ulrich's index, which follows the DJD XXXIX list), attached to no transcribed fragment and to be read in DJD XII, pp. 29–30; so the first confirmed Genesis fragment after 6Q1 is 4Q1 frg. 1 at 22:14, and no scroll covers ch. 7. The commentary 4Q252 is listed as quoting 7:10–8:18; it is a commentary, not a copy of the book, and is outside the project's witness list. The MT is the Leningrad text as archived in sources/wlc-uxlc (UXLC 2.6), accents stripped. The SP was compared verse by verse against the archived consonantal text of MS Dublin Chester Beatty 751 (sources/sp-dt-ucph); von Gall and Tal–Florentin not consulted. The SP differs from the MT in ch. 7 in sense at 7:1 ("God" for YHWH), 7:2 ("male and female" twice for "a man and his woman"; "two two" for "two"), 7:3 ("and also"; "the clean" added), 7:8 ("and from all" for "and all"), 7:9 (YHWH for "God"), 7:16 ("male and female" written twice), and in spelling or form only at 7:2 (הטהרה; היא), 7:3 (the hiphil infinitive for the piel), 7:11 (השש מאות; מעינות; ארבות), 7:13 (no vav before Shem), 7:14 (הם), 7:19 (גבחים for גבהים), 7:23 (no vav before "to flyer"). The LXX is Swete's text (Codex Alexandrinus for Genesis) as archived in sources/lxx-swete-sollupulo and sources/lxx-swete, checked for every verse of the chapter against the Rahlfs–Hanhart text (2006) opened at die-bibel.de in a browser on 2026-10-24; the two agree at every point cited below except that Rahlfs reads egeneto hydatos, "came, of water," at 7:6 where Swete's copy has en, "was," and exanastasin for anastema at 7:4 (two nouns of one sense). The LXX differs from the Hebrew in wording at 7:1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 11, 14, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23. Göttingen not opened. The Vulgate is the Clementine text (sources/vulgate-clementine); the Syriac the ETCBC transcription of the Leiden text (sources/peshitta-etcbc), read in transliteration without a lexicon.

The chapter's names for God: YHWH at 7:1, 5, 16b; Elohim at 7:9, 16a. The SP reverses the first two (Elohim at 7:1, YHWH at 7:9); the Syriac has Alaha, "God," at 7:1 with the SP, and Marya, "the Lord," at 7:5, 16b; the Vulgate has Dominus at 7:1, 5, 9, 16b and Deus at 7:16a; the LXX kyrios ho theos at 7:1, 5, 16b and ho theos at 7:9, 16a. The chapter's numbers: seven (7:2 ×2, 3 ×2, 4, 10), two (7:2, 9 ×2, 15 ×2), forty (7:4 ×2, 12 ×2, 17), six hundred (7:6, 11), seventeen (7:11), three (7:13), fifteen (7:20), a hundred and fifty (7:24).
