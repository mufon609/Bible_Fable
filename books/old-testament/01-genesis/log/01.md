# Genesis 1 — verse log

One section per verse: bold translation line, pointed Hebrew, transliteration, then a table (Hebrew / Transliteration / Literal / Note). Notes state form and usage only. Rules in method/method.md.

## Genesis 1:1

**Translation: In beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.**

בְּרֵאשִׁית בָּרָא אֱלֹהִים אֵת הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֵת הָאָרֶץ

*bereshit bara Elohim et ha-shamayim ve-et ha-aretz*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| בְּרֵאשִׁית | bereshit | in beginning | No definite article ("in **the** beginning" would be *bareshit*). Vowel-dependent: consonants can't settle it; Masoretic vowels and LXX (*en archē*) both lack the article. Masoretic accent is disjunctive, so it stands as its own clause. |
| בָּרָא | bara | created | Perfect tense. Only God is ever the subject of this verb in the Hebrew Bible. |
| אֱלֹהִים | Elohim | God | Plural form, singular verb. Standard Hebrew form for the one God. |
| אֵת | et | — | Direct-object marker; no English equivalent. |
| הַשָּׁמַיִם | ha-shamayim | the heavens | Dual/plural ending; no singular exists in Hebrew. "Heaven" singular is a translator's choice. |
| וְאֵת | ve-et | and \[obj.\] | — |
| הָאָרֶץ | ha-aretz | the earth | Also "the land." |

## Genesis 1:2

**Translation: And the earth was waste and empty, and darkness upon the face of the deep, and the spirit of God hovering upon the face of the waters.**

וְהָאָרֶץ הָיְתָה תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ וְחֹשֶׁךְ עַל־פְּנֵי תְהוֹם וְרוּחַ אֱלֹהִים מְרַחֶפֶת עַל־פְּנֵי הַמָּיִם

*ve-ha-aretz haytah tohu va-vohu ve-choshekh al-penei tehom ve-ruach Elohim merachefet al-penei ha-mayim*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וְהָאָרֶץ הָיְתָה | ve-ha-aretz haytah | and the earth was | *ve-* + noun + perfect = background description, not the next event. Cannot mean "became" (Hebrew would use *hayah l-* or a sequential form). |
| תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ | tohu va-vohu | waste and empty | Rhyming pair. *tohu* = wasteland, emptiness (Deut 32:10, Jer 4:23). *bohu* never occurs without *tohu*. |
| וְחֹשֶׁךְ | ve-choshekh | and darkness | — |
| עַל־פְּנֵי תְהוֹם | al-penei tehom | upon the face of deep | *tehom* has no article: the deep, the abyss of waters. |
| וְרוּחַ אֱלֹהִים | ve-ruach Elohim | and the spirit of God | *ruach* = moving air: wind, breath, spirit. "Spirit" chosen because the verb that follows describes an agent and *ruach Elohim* elsewhere (Gen 41:38, Ex 31:3) is God's spirit. |
| מְרַחֶפֶת | merachefet | hovering | Feminine participle. Only other use of this form: Deut 32:11, an eagle fluttering over its nest (the root also appears once in Jer 23:9, of bones shaking). "Moved" is too weak. |
| עַל־פְּנֵי הַמָּיִם | al-penei ha-mayim | upon the face of the waters | — |

## Genesis 1:3

**Translation: And God said, "Let light be." And light was.**

וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים יְהִי אוֹר וַיְהִי־אוֹר

*vayyomer Elohim yehi or vayhi-or*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים | vayyomer Elohim | and God said | First sequential-narrative verb in the Bible. v.1 is a perfect, v.2 is background; the action chain begins here. |
| יְהִי אוֹר | yehi or | let light be | Jussive. Two words. |
| וַיְהִי־אוֹר | vayhi-or | and light was | Two words, echoing the command exactly. |

## Genesis 1:4

**Translation: And God saw the light, that good. And God separated between the light and between the darkness.**

(Readable form: "saw that the light was good.")

וַיַּרְא אֱלֹהִים אֶת־הָאוֹר כִּי־טוֹב וַיַּבְדֵּל אֱלֹהִים בֵּין הָאוֹר וּבֵין הַחֹשֶׁךְ

*vayyar Elohim et-ha-or ki-tov vayyavdel Elohim bein ha-or u-vein ha-choshekh*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיַּרְא אֱלֹהִים אֶת־הָאוֹר | vayyar Elohim et-ha-or | and God saw the light | — |
| כִּי־טוֹב | ki-tov | that good | No verb "was"; Hebrew drops it. |
| וַיַּבְדֵּל | vayyavdel | and he separated | Causative (hiphil): caused a division. |
| בֵּין הָאוֹר וּבֵין הַחֹשֶׁךְ | bein ha-or u-vein ha-choshekh | between the light and between the darkness | Hebrew repeats "between"; English translations collapse it. |

## Genesis 1:5

**Translation: And God called the light "Day," and the darkness he called "Night." And there was evening and there was morning: day one.**

וַיִּקְרָא אֱלֹהִים לָאוֹר יוֹם וְלַחֹשֶׁךְ קָרָא לָיְלָה וַיְהִי־עֶרֶב וַיְהִי־בֹקֶר יוֹם אֶחָד

*vayyiqra Elohim la-or yom ve-la-choshekh qara laylah vayhi-erev vayhi-voqer yom echad*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיִּקְרָא אֱלֹהִים לָאוֹר יוֹם | vayyiqra Elohim la-or yom | and God called to the light "Day" | — |
| וְלַחֹשֶׁךְ קָרָא לָיְלָה | ve-la-choshekh qara laylah | and to the darkness he called "Night" | Word order shifts; subject not repeated. |
| וַיְהִי־עֶרֶב וַיְהִי־בֹקֶר | vayhi-erev vayhi-voqer | and evening was, and morning was | — |
| יוֹם אֶחָד | yom echad | day one | Cardinal number, not ordinal. "The first day" would be *yom ha-rishon*. Days 2–5 use ordinals; days 6 and 7 add the article. LXX agrees: *hēmera mia*, "day one." |

## Genesis 1:6

**Translation: And God said, "Let an expanse be in the midst of the waters, and let it be a separator between waters and waters."**

וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים יְהִי רָקִיעַ בְּתוֹךְ הַמָּיִם וִיהִי מַבְדִּיל בֵּין מַיִם לָמָיִם

*vayyomer Elohim yehi raqia betokh ha-mayim vihi mavdil bein mayim la-mayim*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| יְהִי רָקִיעַ | yehi raqia | let an expanse be | *raqia* is from the verb *raqa*, to beat out or hammer thin (Ex 39:3, gold beaten into plates; Isa 40:19; Job 37:18, the skies spread out hard as a cast mirror). The noun means a beaten-out sheet. LXX *stereōma* ("firm thing") → Latin *firmamentum* → KJV "firmament." No article: "an expanse." |
| בְּתוֹךְ הַמָּיִם | betokh ha-mayim | in the midst of the waters | — |
| וִיהִי מַבְדִּיל | vihi mavdil | and let it be a separator | Hiphil participle: a thing that divides. Same root as 1:4. |
| בֵּין מַיִם לָמָיִם | bein mayim la-mayim | between waters to waters | No articles; Hebrew idiom for "between one set of waters and another." |

## Genesis 1:7

**Translation: And God made the expanse, and separated between the waters which are from beneath the expanse and between the waters which are from above the expanse. And it was so.**

וַיַּעַשׂ אֱלֹהִים אֶת־הָרָקִיעַ וַיַּבְדֵּל בֵּין הַמַּיִם אֲשֶׁר מִתַּחַת לָרָקִיעַ וּבֵין הַמַּיִם אֲשֶׁר מֵעַל לָרָקִיעַ וַיְהִי־כֵן

*vayya'as Elohim et-ha-raqia vayyavdel bein ha-mayim asher mittachat la-raqia u-vein ha-mayim asher me'al la-raqia vayhi-khen*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיַּעַשׂ | vayya'as | and he made | *asah*, the ordinary verb for making. Not *bara* (1:1). The text distinguishes the two. |
| אֶת־הָרָקִיעַ | et-ha-raqia | the expanse | Now with the article: the one just spoken. |
| מִתַּחַת לָרָקִיעַ | mittachat la-raqia | from beneath, to the expanse | Compound preposition: "under the expanse." |
| מֵעַל לָרָקִיעַ | me'al la-raqia | from above, to the expanse | "Above the expanse." Waters sit on top of it. |
| וַיְהִי־כֵן | vayhi-khen | and it was so | **Textual variant.** The Masoretic Text and the Dead Sea Scrolls place this at the end of v.7, after the making. The LXX moves it to the end of v.6, after the command, and v.7 ends without it. The LXX pattern is more uniform (command → "it was so" → execution); the Hebrew witnesses are older and agree with each other, so the log follows them. |

## Genesis 1:8

**Translation: And God called the expanse "Heavens." And there was evening and there was morning: a second day.**

וַיִּקְרָא אֱלֹהִים לָרָקִיעַ שָׁמָיִם וַיְהִי־עֶרֶב וַיְהִי־בֹקֶר יוֹם שֵׁנִי

*vayyiqra Elohim la-raqia shamayim vayhi-erev vayhi-voqer yom sheni*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיִּקְרָא אֱלֹהִים לָרָקִיעַ שָׁמָיִם | vayyiqra Elohim la-raqia shamayim | and God called to the expanse "Heavens" | Same word as 1:1, no article. **The expanse of 1:6–7 is what the text calls *shamayim*.** The "heavens" of 1:1 are, in the narrative's own terms, this beaten-out expanse holding back the upper waters. KJV: "Heaven." |
| יוֹם שֵׁנִי | yom sheni | a second day | Ordinal, no article (contrast *yom echad*, 1:5, and *yom ha-shishi*, 1:31). KJV: "the second day." |
| — | — | — | **Textual variant.** Day two has no "and God saw that it was good" in the MT or DSS. The LXX adds the phrase at the end of v.8. The Hebrew witnesses agree; the log omits it. |

## Genesis 1:9

**Translation: And God said, "Let the waters from under the heavens be gathered to one place, and let the dry \[ground\] be seen." And it was so.**

וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים יִקָּווּ הַמַּיִם מִתַּחַת הַשָּׁמַיִם אֶל־מָקוֹם אֶחָד וְתֵרָאֶה הַיַּבָּשָׁה וַיְהִי־כֵן

*vayyomer Elohim yiqqavu ha-mayim mittachat ha-shamayim el-maqom echad ve-tera'eh ha-yabbashah vayhi-khen*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| יִקָּווּ הַמַּיִם | yiqqavu ha-mayim | let the waters be gathered | Passive (niphal) of *qavah*, to collect; the noun *miqveh* in v.10 is from the same root. |
| מִתַּחַת הַשָּׁמַיִם | mittachat ha-shamayim | from under the heavens | The lower waters of v.7. |
| אֶל־מָקוֹם אֶחָד | el-maqom echad | to one place | — |
| וְתֵרָאֶה הַיַּבָּשָׁה | ve-tera'eh ha-yabbashah | and let the dry be seen | *yabbashah* is "the dry" used as a noun: dry ground. Passive: it appears; it is not made. |
| וַיְהִי־כֵן | vayhi-khen | and it was so | Here, unlike v.7, the formula follows the command. **Variant:** the LXX adds after it a report that the waters gathered and the dry land appeared. The Hebrew witnesses lack it; it is the same harmonizing hand as in vv.6–8. |

## Genesis 1:10

**Translation: And God called the dry \[ground\] "Earth," and the gathering of the waters he called "Seas." And God saw that good.**

וַיִּקְרָא אֱלֹהִים לַיַּבָּשָׁה אֶרֶץ וּלְמִקְוֵה הַמַּיִם קָרָא יַמִּים וַיַּרְא אֱלֹהִים כִּי־טוֹב

*vayyiqra Elohim la-yabbashah eretz u-le-miqveh ha-mayim qara yammim vayyar Elohim ki-tov*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| לַיַּבָּשָׁה אֶרֶץ | la-yabbashah eretz | to the dry, "Earth" | **Same word as 1:1.** *eretz* is now defined by the text itself as the dry ground, as *shamayim* was defined in 1:8 as the expanse. |
| וּלְמִקְוֵה הַמַּיִם | u-le-miqveh ha-mayim | and to the gathering of the waters | — |
| יַמִּים | yammim | Seas | Plural. KJV keeps it. |
| כִּי־טוֹב | ki-tov | that good | First of two on day three. |

## Genesis 1:11

**Translation: And God said, "Let the earth sprout sprouts: herb seeding seed, fruit tree making fruit to its kind, whose seed is in it, upon the earth." And it was so.**

וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים תַּדְשֵׁא הָאָרֶץ דֶּשֶׁא עֵשֶׂב מַזְרִיעַ זֶרַע עֵץ פְּרִי עֹשֶׂה פְּרִי לְמִינוֹ אֲשֶׁר זַרְעוֹ־בוֹ עַל־הָאָרֶץ וַיְהִי־כֵן

*vayyomer Elohim tadshe ha-aretz deshe esev mazria zera etz peri oseh peri le-mino asher zar'o-vo al-ha-aretz vayhi-khen*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| תַּדְשֵׁא הָאָרֶץ דֶּשֶׁא | tadshe ha-aretz deshe | let the earth sprout sprouts | Verb and noun from one root (a cognate accusative). The earth is the one commanded to act. |
| עֵשֶׂב מַזְרִיעַ זֶרַע | esev mazria zera | herb seeding seed | Again verb and noun from one root: plants that produce seed. |
| עֵץ פְּרִי עֹשֶׂה פְּרִי | etz peri oseh peri | fruit tree making fruit | — |
| לְמִינוֹ | le-mino | to its kind | *min*, kind, sort. In v.11 it is attached only to the tree; in v.12 to both. The phrase states that plants carry their own seed and reproduce their own sort; it says nothing further. KJV: "after his kind." |
| אֲשֶׁר זַרְעוֹ־בוֹ | asher zar'o-vo | which its seed \[is\] in it | The fruit contains the seed. |
| וַיְהִי־כֵן | vayhi-khen | and it was so | **Variant:** LXX adds "and according to likeness" after "kind" in vv.11–12. Hebrew witnesses lack it. |

## Genesis 1:12

**Translation: And the earth brought out sprouts: herb seeding seed to its kind, and tree making fruit whose seed is in it, to its kind. And God saw that good.**

וַתּוֹצֵא הָאָרֶץ דֶּשֶׁא עֵשֶׂב מַזְרִיעַ זֶרַע לְמִינֵהוּ וְעֵץ עֹשֶׂה־פְּרִי אֲשֶׁר זַרְעוֹ־בוֹ לְמִינֵהוּ וַיַּרְא אֱלֹהִים כִּי־טוֹב

*vattotze ha-aretz deshe esev mazria zera le-minehu ve-etz oseh-peri asher zar'o-vo le-minehu vayyar Elohim ki-tov*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַתּוֹצֵא הָאָרֶץ | vattotze ha-aretz | and the earth brought out | Causative of *yatza*, to go out. **The earth is the grammatical subject.** God speaks in v.11; the earth produces in v.12. This is the first act of creation the text assigns to something other than God. |
| לְמִינֵהוּ | le-minehu | to its kind | Now on both herb and tree. |
| כִּי־טוֹב | ki-tov | that good | Second on day three. |

## Genesis 1:14

**Translation: And God said, "Let lights be in the expanse of the heavens, to separate between the day and between the night; and let them be for signs and for appointed times and for days and years."**

וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים יְהִי מְאֹרֹת בִּרְקִיעַ הַשָּׁמַיִם לְהַבְדִּיל בֵּין הַיּוֹם וּבֵין הַלָּיְלָה וְהָיוּ לְאֹתֹת וּלְמוֹעֲדִים וּלְיָמִים וְשָׁנִים

*vayyomer Elohim yehi me'orot bi-rqia ha-shamayim le-havdil bein ha-yom u-vein ha-laylah ve-hayu le-otot u-le-mo'adim u-le-yamim ve-shanim*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| יְהִי מְאֹרֹת | yehi me'orot | let lights be | *ma'or* = a light-bearer, a lamp (same word for the tabernacle lamps, Ex 35:14). Not *or*, "light," of 1:3. The verb is singular before a plural subject, ordinary Hebrew. |
| בִּרְקִיעַ הַשָּׁמַיִם | bi-rqia ha-shamayim | in the expanse of the heavens | The *raqia* of 1:6–8. The lights are placed inside the beaten-out sheet, below the upper waters of 1:7. |
| לְהַבְדִּיל בֵּין… וּבֵין | le-havdil bein… u-vein | to separate between… and between | Doubled "between" again. |
| לְאֹתֹת | le-otot | for signs | *ot*, a sign or marker (the rainbow, Gen 9:12; the Sabbath, Ex 31:13). |
| וּלְמוֹעֲדִים | u-le-mo'adim | and for appointed times | *mo'ed* = an appointed time or meeting. It is the word for the festival calendar (Lev 23:2, "the appointed times of YHWH") and for the tent of meeting (*ohel mo'ed*). Not the four seasons; Hebrew has other words for those (Gen 8:22). KJV: "seasons." |
| וּלְיָמִים וְשָׁנִים | u-le-yamim ve-shanim | and for days and years | — |
| — | — | — | **Variant:** LXX adds "for illumination of the earth" after "heavens," copied from v.15. Hebrew witnesses lack it. |

*ve-hayu li-m'orot bi-rqia ha-shamayim le-ha'ir al-ha-aretz vayhi-khen*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| לְהָאִיר עַל־הָאָרֶץ | le-ha'ir al-ha-aretz | to give light upon the earth | Causative of *or*: to cause light. Their stated purpose. |

## Genesis 1:16

**Translation: And God made the two great lights: the great light for the rule of the day, and the small light for the rule of the night, and the stars.**

וַיַּעַשׂ אֱלֹהִים אֶת־שְׁנֵי הַמְּאֹרֹת הַגְּדֹלִים אֶת־הַמָּאוֹר הַגָּדֹל לְמֶמְשֶׁלֶת הַיּוֹם וְאֶת־הַמָּאוֹר הַקָּטֹן לְמֶמְשֶׁלֶת הַלַּיְלָה וְאֵת הַכּוֹכָבִים

*vayya'as Elohim et-shnei ha-me'orot ha-gedolim et-ha-ma'or ha-gadol le-memshelet ha-yom ve-et-ha-ma'or ha-qaton le-memshelet ha-laylah ve-et ha-kokhavim*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיַּעַשׂ | vayya'as | and he made | *asah*, as in 1:7. |

## Genesis 1:26

**Translation: And God said, "Let us make human in our image, as our likeness; and let them rule over the fish of the sea and over the flyer of the heavens and over the beast and over all the earth and over every creeper that creeps upon the earth."**

וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים נַעֲשֶׂה אָדָם בְּצַלְמֵנוּ כִּדְמוּתֵנוּ וְיִרְדּוּ בִדְגַת הַיָּם וּבְעוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם וּבַבְּהֵמָה וּבְכָל־הָאָרֶץ וּבְכָל־הָרֶמֶשׂ הָרֹמֵשׂ עַל־הָאָרֶץ

*vayyomer Elohim na'aseh adam be-tzalmenu ki-dmutenu ve-yirdu vi-dgat ha-yam u-ve-of ha-shamayim u-va-behemah u-ve-khol-ha-aretz u-ve-khol-ha-remes ha-romes al-ha-aretz*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| נַעֲשֶׂה | na'aseh | let us make | First-person **plural**. The making in v.27 is singular (*bara*, he created). Biblical Hebrew has no royal "we" for verbs; kings speak in the singular. The same plural appears in 3:22 ("like one of us"), 11:7 ("let us go down") and Isa 6:8 ("who will go for us"), where in Isa 6 the seraphim are present. The grammar is an address to others present; who they are, the verse does not say. |
| אָדָם | adam | human | **No article; a collective noun, not a name.** Built on *adamah* (v.25): the ground-creature. The pronoun that follows is plural ("let them rule"). KJV: "man." |
| בְּצַלְמֵנוּ | be-tzalmenu | in our image | *tzelem* = image, statue. It is the ordinary word for a cult statue (Num 33:52, 2 Kgs 11:18, Amos 5:26). |
| כִּדְמוּתֵנוּ | ki-dmutenu | as our likeness | *demut* = resemblance, likeness (Ezek 1:5, 26). Different preposition from the one before: "in" image, "as" likeness. |
| וְיִרְדּוּ | ve-yirdu | and let them rule | *radah*: to rule, hold sway, tread (Lev 25:43 warns against ruling with harshness; Ps 72:8 of a king). Plural subject: the collective *adam*. KJV: "have dominion." |
| וּבְכָל־הָאָרֶץ | u-ve-khol-ha-aretz | and over all the earth | **Variant:** the Syriac Peshitta reads "over every living-thing of the earth," completing the list; MT, LXX and SP all read "all the earth." The log follows the three older witnesses. |
