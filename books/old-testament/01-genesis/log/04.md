# Genesis 4 — verse log

One section per verse: bold translation line, pointed Hebrew, transliteration, then a table (Hebrew / Transliteration / Literal / Note). Notes state form and usage only. Rules in method/method.md.

Witnesses for this chapter: MT, SP and LXX throughout; Syriac and Vulgate cited where they differ. The one Dead Sea Scroll listed for the chapter is 4QGen-b, given as 4:2–4 in one published list and 4:2–11 in another (DJD XII, pp. 31–38, not consulted); it is cited at 4:2 and 4:4 only, and its letters are reported as matching the MT where legible, plates not inspected. The SP consonants were compared verse by verse with the MT on 2026-10-24 (text as presented at tanakh.info; von Gall's apparatus not consulted); the SP differs from the MT in ch. 4 at 4:4 (spelling), 4:7 (spelling), 4:8 (a clause added), 4:10 (number of a participle), 4:12 (spelling), 4:17 (an et added), 4:18 (spelling of a name), 4:22 (spelling of the pronoun), 4:23 (spelling), 4:25 (he/she called) and 4:26 (the stem of "begin").

## Genesis 4:1

**Translation: And the human knew Chavvah his woman, and she conceived and bore Qayin; and she said, "I have acquired a man with YHWH."**

וְהָאָדָם יָדַע אֶת־חַוָּה אִשְׁתּוֹ וַתַּהַר וַתֵּלֶד אֶת־קַיִן וַתֹּאמֶר קָנִיתִי אִישׁ אֶת־יְהוָה

*ve-ha-adam yada et-Chavvah ishto vattahar vatteled et-Qayin vattomer qaniti ish et-YHWH*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וְהָאָדָם יָדַע | ve-ha-adam yada | and the human knew | ve- + noun + perfect: background, as 1:2; 2:5; 3:1. The article is in the consonants (האדם), as in 3:8–24; the vowels are not involved. yada, the verb of 3:5, 7, 22, here of a man and a woman: Gen 4:17, 25; 19:8; 24:16; 38:26; Judg 11:39; 1 Kgs 1:4. KJV: "Adam knew." |
| חַוָּה | Chavvah | Chavvah | Second and last use of the name (3:20). |
| וַתַּהַר וַתֵּלֶד | vattahar vatteled | and she conceived and bore | harah, to conceive (the noun heron, 3:16); yalad, to bear. The pair recurs in 4:17 and through the genealogies (21:2; 29:32–35). |
| קַיִן | Qayin | Qayin | The name; the next word, qaniti, is sounded on it. qayin as a common noun is a spear (2 Sam 21:16). KJV: "Cain." |
| קָנִיתִי אִישׁ | qaniti ish | I have acquired a man | qanah, to acquire, buy, get (Gen 25:10, a field; 47:19–23, land and people for bread; Prov 4:5, wisdom; Deut 32:6 and Ps 139:13, of God). ish, "man," the word of 2:23–24 and 3:6, 16, used of the newborn. KJV: "I have gotten a man." |
| אֶת־יְהוָה | et-YHWH | with YHWH | et is both the object marker and the preposition "with" (Gen 5:22, 24, "walked with God"; 6:9). After a verb that already has its object (ish), it is "with." LXX: dia tou theou, "through God"; Vulgate: per Deum. SP: et-YHWH, as MT. The sense "from" has no word here; "from" is min. KJV: "from the LORD." |

## Genesis 4:2

**Translation: And she again bore his brother, Hevel. And Hevel was a shepherd of flock, and Qayin was a worker of ground.**

וַתֹּסֶף לָלֶדֶת אֶת־אָחִיו אֶת־הָבֶל וַיְהִי־הֶבֶל רֹעֵה צֹאן וְקַיִן הָיָה עֹבֵד אֲדָמָה

*vattosef laledet et-achiv et-Havel vayhi-Hevel ro'eh tzon ve-Qayin hayah oved adamah*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַתֹּסֶף לָלֶדֶת | vattosef laledet | and she again bore | yasaf + infinitive, "added to bear": the Hebrew way of saying "again" (4:12; 8:10, 12, 21; 18:29; 38:26). No second conception is stated. |
| אֶת־אָחִיו אֶת־הָבֶל | et-achiv et-Havel | his brother, Hevel | ach, brother: first use. It occurs seven times in 4:2–11. The name is given without a gloss; hevel as a common noun is breath, vapor (Isa 57:13; Eccl 1:2, "vanity"). The text does not connect them. KJV: "Abel." |
| רֹעֵה צֹאן | ro'eh tzon | shepherd of flock | ra'ah, to pasture; participle as a noun. tzon, flock of sheep and goats (29:2; 30:31). The first occupation named. |
| עֹבֵד אֲדָמָה | oved adamah | worker of ground | avad + adamah, the phrase of 2:5 ("to work the ground") and 3:23, as a participle without the article. 4QGen-b is listed as beginning at this verse; its letters are reported as matching the MT where legible; plates not inspected. |

## Genesis 4:3

**Translation: And it was, at the end of days, and Qayin brought from the fruit of the ground an offering to YHWH.**

וַיְהִי מִקֵּץ יָמִים וַיָּבֵא קַיִן מִפְּרִי הָאֲדָמָה מִנְחָה לַיהוָה

*vayhi miqqetz yamim vayyave Qayin mi-peri ha-adamah minchah la-YHWH*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| מִקֵּץ יָמִים | miqqetz yamim | at the end of days | qetz, end; "at the end of days" is an unspecified interval (1 Kgs 17:7; Neh 13:6, "at the end of days I asked leave"). Not "the end of days" in the sense of a last age, which is qetz ha-yamim with the article or acharit ha-yamim (Gen 49:1; Dan 12:13). KJV: "in process of time." |
| וַיָּבֵא | vayyave | and he brought | bo (hiphil), to bring: the verb of 2:19, 22 (God bringing animals and the woman to the human). |
| מִפְּרִי הָאֲדָמָה | mi-peri ha-adamah | from the fruit of the ground | peri, the fruit of 1:11–12, 29; 3:2–3, 6; adamah, the ground of 2:7 and 3:17–19, 23, now yielding. |
| מִנְחָה | minchah | an offering | A gift carried to a superior: Jacob's to Esau (32:14, 19, 21–22; 33:10), the brothers' to Joseph (43:11, 15, 25–26), Ehud's to Eglon (Judg 3:15–18), tribute from subject nations (2 Sam 8:2, 6; 1 Kgs 4:21; 2 Kgs 17:3–4). In the law it is the grain offering (Lev 2:1–16; 6:14–23) set beside the animal offerings; in 1 Sam 2:17, 29 and Mal 1:10–13 it covers offerings generally. About 211 uses; see Concordance. No altar, no verb "sacrifice" (zavach) and no "sacrifice" (zevach) occur in the chapter; the altar first appears at 8:20. KJV: "offering." |

## Genesis 4:4

**Translation: And Hevel brought, also he, from the firstborn of his flock and from their fat; and YHWH looked to Hevel and to his offering;**

וְהֶבֶל הֵבִיא גַם־הוּא מִבְּכֹרוֹת צֹאנוֹ וּמֵחֶלְבֵהֶן וַיִּשַׁע יְהוָה אֶל־הֶבֶל וְאֶל־מִנְחָתוֹ

*ve-Hevel hevi gam-hu mi-bekhorot tzono u-me-chelvehen vayyisha YHWH el-Hevel ve-el-minchato*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| הֵבִיא גַם־הוּא | hevi gam-hu | brought, also he | The same verb as 4:3, with gam-hu, "he too" (as 4:22, 26 of Tzillah and Shet). The two offerings are introduced with one verb. |
| מִבְּכֹרוֹת צֹאנוֹ | mi-bekhorot tzono | from the firstborn of his flock | bekhorah, firstborn (feminine plural of the animals); the firstborn of flock and herd are YHWH's in Ex 13:12; 22:29–30; Deut 15:19. |
| וּמֵחֶלְבֵהֶן | u-me-chelvehen | and from their fat | chelev, fat, the portion burned on the altar in Lev 3:3–4, 16 ("all fat is YHWH's"), also "the best" (Num 18:12, "the fat of the oil"; Ps 81:16). Suffix feminine plural, agreeing with the firstborn. SP spells it with a yod (ומחלביהן); same word. KJV: "the fat thereof." |
| וַיִּשַׁע אֶל | vayyisha el | and he looked to | sha'ah, to look toward, gaze at, regard: Ex 5:9 ("let them not look to lying words"); Isa 17:7–8 (a man "will look to his maker" and not to the altars); 31:1 (they "do not look to the Holy One"); 22:4 ("look away from me"); Ps 119:117; Job 7:19; 14:6. About 15 uses; see Concordance. The verse states the looking and in 4:5 the not looking; it gives no ground for either. LXX: epeiden, "looked upon"; Vulgate: respexit. 4QGen-b is listed as covering this verse; reported as matching the MT where legible; plates not inspected. KJV: "had respect unto." |

## Genesis 4:5

**Translation: but to Qayin and to his offering he did not look. And it burned to Qayin greatly, and his face fell.**

וְאֶל־קַיִן וְאֶל־מִנְחָתוֹ לֹא שָׁעָה וַיִּחַר לְקַיִן מְאֹד וַיִּפְּלוּ פָּנָיו

*ve-el-Qayin ve-el-minchato lo sha'ah vayyichar le-Qayin me'od vayyippelu panav*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| לֹא שָׁעָה | lo sha'ah | he did not look | The verb of 4:4 negated; the object is again both the man and the offering. |
| וַיִּחַר לְקַיִן | vayyichar le-Qayin | and it burned to Qayin | charah, to be hot; impersonal with le-, "it was hot to him": anger (Gen 31:36; 34:7; 45:5; Num 16:15; 1 Sam 15:11; Jonah 4:1, 4, 9). The first anger in the Bible. KJV: "Cain was very wroth." |
| וַיִּפְּלוּ פָּנָיו | vayyippelu panav | and his face fell | nafal, to fall; panim, face (plural in form). The phrase occurs only here and 4:6; its opposite, a face lifted, is in 4:7 (se'et) and Job 11:15; 22:26. KJV: "his countenance fell." |

## Genesis 4:7

**Translation: Is it not: if you do well, a lifting; and if you do not do well, at the door sin \[is\] lying down; and to you its desire, and you shall rule over it."**

הֲלוֹא אִם־תֵּיטִיב שְׂאֵת וְאִם לֹא תֵיטִיב לַפֶּתַח חַטָּאת רֹבֵץ וְאֵלֶיךָ תְּשׁוּקָתוֹ וְאַתָּה תִּמְשָׁל־בּוֹ

*halo im-tetiv se'et ve-im lo tetiv la-petach chatta't rovetz ve-eleikha teshuqato ve-attah timshol-bo*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| הֲלוֹא | halo | is it not | ha- interrogative + lo: "is it not so that…" (Gen 13:9; 20:5; 29:25). What follows has no main verb. |
| אִם־תֵּיטִיב | im-tetiv | if you do well | yatav (hiphil), to do well, do good (Gen 12:13; 32:9; Lev 5:4; Deut 5:28, "they have done well in what they have said"). SP spells it without the first yod (תטיב), twice; same form. |
| שְׂאֵת | se'et | a lifting | Infinitive construct of nasa, to lift, carry, bear, standing alone as a noun: "lifting" (Gen 49:3, se'et as "dignity"; Hab 1:7; Job 13:11; 31:23). nasa is used of lifting the face (Job 11:15; 22:26; 2 Sam 2:22), carrying a load (Gen 45:23), and lifting away guilt (Ex 34:7; Num 14:18; Ps 32:1, nesui pesha). The same verb closes 4:13 (minneso). The clause has no verb: "if you do well, lifting." LXX reads the whole opening as "if you offer rightly but do not divide rightly, have you not sinned? Be still"; Syriac: "accepted"; Vulgate: recipies, "you shall receive." Added to Judging variants. KJV: "shalt thou not be accepted?" |
| לַפֶּתַח | la-petach | at the door | petach, opening, doorway (18:1; 19:6, 11; Ex 12:22–23, the doorway the blood is put on). With the article: the door, none having been mentioned. |
| חַטָּאת | chatta't | sin | First use of the word in the Bible. Feminine noun: a missing, an offense (Gen 18:20; 31:36; 50:17; Ex 32:30–34), and in the law the offering for one (Lev 4:3, 8, 14). About 290 uses. The verb chata first appears at 20:6, 9. |
| רֹבֵץ | rovetz | lying down | ravatz, to lie down, stretch out, of animals: flocks at a well (29:2), a lion (49:9), a donkey under its load (Ex 23:5; Num 22:27), a bird on its nest (Deut 22:6); of the deep lying beneath (49:25; Deut 33:13); of a curse that "lies on" a man (Deut 29:19, ve-ravtzah bo). About 30 uses; see Concordance. Masculine singular participle; chatta't is feminine, so the participle does not agree with it in gender. The two words stand side by side without a verb. SP: rovetz. LXX: hesychason, "be still." KJV: "sin lieth." |
| וְאֵלֶיךָ תְּשׁוּקָתוֹ | ve-eleikha teshuqato | and to you its desire | teshuqah, the word of 3:16 (3 uses; see Concordance), with a masculine suffix: its referent is masculine, as the participle is, not the feminine noun. The construction is that of 3:16 word for word: el- + suffix, teshuqah + suffix. SP reads teshuqato with the MT (checked 2026-10-24); LXX: apostrophe autou, "his turning," as at 3:16; Syriac: "turn toward it"; Vulgate: appetitus eius, "its appetite." |
| וְאַתָּה תִּמְשָׁל־בּוֹ | ve-attah timshol-bo | and you shall rule over it | mashal be-, the verb and preposition of 3:16 (ve-hu yimshol-bakh) and 1:18. Imperfect, the form of 3:16's "he shall rule" and 3:18's "it shall sprout." The pronoun attah is added before the verb, as in 3:15 (ve-attah teshufennu). The suffix bo is masculine, with teshuqato and rovetz. KJV: "thou shalt rule over him." |

## Genesis 4:8

**Translation: And Qayin said to Hevel his brother. And it was, in their being in the field, and Qayin rose up against Hevel his brother and killed him.**

וַיֹּאמֶר קַיִן אֶל־הֶבֶל אָחִיו וַיְהִי בִּהְיוֹתָם בַּשָּׂדֶה וַיָּקָם קַיִן אֶל־הֶבֶל אָחִיו וַיַּהַרְגֵהוּ

*vayyomer Qayin el-Hevel achiv vayhi bihyotam ba-sadeh vayyaqom Qayin el-Hevel achiv vayyahargehu*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיֹּאמֶר קַיִן אֶל־הֶבֶל אָחִיו | vayyomer Qayin el-Hevel achiv | and Qayin said to Hevel his brother | amar, "said," with no words following. In the MT the next word is vayhi, "and it was." SP adds נלכה השדה, nelekhah ha-sadeh, "let us go to the field"; LXX dielthomen eis to pedion, Syriac and Vulgate (egrediamur foras, "let us go outside") have the same clause; the Targums also have it. Four witnesses, one Hebrew, against the MT. Whether 4QGen-b preserves this point of the verse is unchecked (DJD XII). Added to Judging variants. KJV: "talked with Abel his brother." |
| בִּהְיוֹתָם בַּשָּׂדֶה | bihyotam ba-sadeh | in their being in the field | sadeh, the field of 2:5, 19–20; 3:1, 14, 18, with the article. |
| וַיָּקָם… אֶל | vayyaqom… el | and he rose up… against | qum el-, to rise against someone (Deut 19:11; 22:26; Judg 9:18; 20:5; 1 Sam 22:13; 24:7). |
| וַיַּהַרְגֵהוּ | vayyahargehu | and killed him | harag, to kill, slay (4:14, 15, 23, 25; 12:12; 20:4; Ex 2:14–15). The ordinary verb for killing a person; not ratzach of the commandment (Ex 20:13). The first death in the Bible. |

## Genesis 4:9

**Translation: And YHWH said to Qayin, "Where is Hevel your brother?" And he said, "I do not know. A keeper of my brother \[am\] I?"**

וַיֹּאמֶר יְהוָה אֶל־קַיִן אֵי הֶבֶל אָחִיךָ וַיֹּאמֶר לֹא יָדַעְתִּי הֲשֹׁמֵר אָחִי אָנֹכִי

*vayyomer YHWH el-Qayin ei Hevel achikha vayyomer lo yada'ti ha-shomer achi anokhi*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| אֵי הֶבֶל אָחִיךָ | ei Hevel achikha | where is Hevel your brother | ei, "where": this short form occurs in Genesis only here; 18:9 and 19:5 have ayyeh. A question about a person's place, as ayyekkah in 3:9. |
| לֹא יָדַעְתִּי | lo yada'ti | I do not know | yada, the verb of 4:1 and 3:5, 7, 22; perfect used for a present state. |
| הֲשֹׁמֵר אָחִי אָנֹכִי | ha-shomer achi anokhi | a keeper of my brother \[am\] I | ha- interrogative (not the article) on the participle of shamar: the verb of 2:15 ("to keep it") and 3:24 (the cherubim "to keep the way"). shomer as a noun is a watchman or guard (Judg 1:24; 1 Sam 17:20, of the camp; 2 Kgs 11:5–7; Ps 121:4, of YHWH keeping Israel; 127:1). Of persons as object: Gen 28:15, 20; 1 Sam 26:15; Ps 16:1. No verb; anokhi, the long form of "I" (3:10). KJV: "Am I my brother's keeper?" |

## Genesis 4:10

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| קוֹל דְּמֵי אָחִיךָ | qol demei achikha | the voice of the bloods of your brother | qol, the sound or voice of 3:8, 10, 17. dam, blood, here plural (damim), the form used of blood that is shed (Gen 37:26; Ex 22:2; 2 Sam 16:8; 2 Kgs 9:26; Hos 4:2). ## Genesis 4:12

**Translation: When you work the ground, it shall not again give its strength to you; staggering and wandering you shall be in the earth."**

## Genesis 4:24

**Translation: For sevenfold shall Qayin be avenged, and Lemekh seventy and seven."**

כִּי שִׁבְעָתַיִם יֻקַּם־קָיִן וְלֶמֶךְ שִׁבְעִים וְשִׁבְעָה

*ki shiv'atayim yuqqam-Qayin ve-Lemekh shiv'im ve-shiv'ah*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| שִׁבְעָתַיִם יֻקַּם־קָיִן | shiv'atayim yuqqam-Qayin | sevenfold shall Qayin be avenged | The words of 4:15 (shiv'atayim yuqqam) quoted with Qayin as the subject of the passive. |
| שִׁבְעִים וְשִׁבְעָה | shiv'im ve-shiv'ah | seventy and seven | A number, "seventy and seven," set against "sevenfold"; no verb and no "-fold." LXX: hebdomekontakis hepta, "seventy times seven" (the phrase of Matt 18:22). KJV: "seventy and sevenfold." |

## Genesis 4:25

**Translation: And Adam knew again his woman, and she bore a son, and she called his name Shet, "for God has set for me another seed instead of Hevel, for Qayin killed him."**

וַיֵּדַע אָדָם עוֹד אֶת־אִשְׁתּוֹ וַתֵּלֶד בֵּן וַתִּקְרָא אֶת־שְׁמוֹ שֵׁת כִּי שָׁת־לִי אֱלֹהִים זֶרַע אַחֵר תַּחַת הֶבֶל כִּי הֲרָגוֹ קָיִן

*vayyeda Adam od et-ishto vatteled ben vattiqra et-shemo Shet ki shat-li Elohim zera acher tachat Hevel ki harago Qayin*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיֵּדַע אָדָם | vayyeda Adam | and Adam knew | אדם without the article, in the consonants, as the subject of a verb. Every earlier case without the article was after the preposition le- (2:20; 3:17, 21), where the consonants cannot show one; in 4:1 the consonants have it (והאדם). This is the first place in the story where the letters themselves read the word as a name. SP and LXX (Adam) agree. The log renders "Adam" here and "the human" where the article is present. KJV: "Adam." |
| עוֹד | od | again | od, "again," "still," here in place of the yasaf construction of 4:2. |
| וַתִּקְרָא אֶת־שְׁמוֹ | vattiqra et-shemo | and she called his name | Feminine: the woman names, as in 4:1 she spoke the name's reason. SP: vayyiqra, "and he called" (one letter, ת/י). LXX: epōnomasen… legousa, with a feminine "saying." Added to Judging variants. The man names in 4:26 (vayyiqra) in all witnesses. |
| שֵׁת כִּי שָׁת־לִי | Shet ki shat-li | Shet, for he has set for me | shit, to set, place, the verb of 3:15 (ashit, "I will set enmity"). The name שת and the verb שת have the same two consonants; the vowels differ. KJV: "Seth: For God… hath appointed me." |
| אֱלֹהִים | Elohim | God | The woman says Elohim here; in 4:1 she said YHWH. The narrator of ch. 4 says YHWH throughout (4:3, 4, 6, 9, 13, 15, 16, 26). |
| זֶרַע אַחֵר תַּחַת הֶבֶל | zera acher tachat Hevel | another seed instead of Hevel | zera, the seed of 1:11–12, 29; 3:15, here of one child. tachat, "in place of" (2:21, "in its place"; 22:13, the ram "instead of his son"). |
| כִּי הֲרָגוֹ קָיִן | ki harago Qayin | for Qayin killed him | harag, fifth and last use in the chapter. The mother states the killing in her naming speech. |

## Genesis 4:26

**Translation: And to Shet, also he, was born a son, and he called his name Enosh. Then it was begun to call on the name of YHWH.**

וּלְשֵׁת גַּם־הוּא יֻלַּד־בֵּן וַיִּקְרָא אֶת־שְׁמוֹ אֱנוֹשׁ אָז הוּחַל לִקְרֹא בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה

*u-le-Shet gam-hu yullad-ben vayyiqra et-shemo Enosh az huchal liqro be-shem YHWH*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| גַּם־הוּא יֻלַּד־בֵּן | gam-hu yullad-ben | also he, was born a son | yalad (pual), passive, "there was born"; gam-hu as 4:4, 22. |
| אֱנוֹשׁ | Enosh | Enosh | A common noun for a human being, in poetry (Deut 32:26; Job 7:17; 25:6; Ps 8:5, "what is enosh"; 90:3; 103:15; Isa 51:12), used as a name. Like adam, it is both a word for humankind and a name in the genealogy (5:6–11). KJV: "Enos." |
| אָז הוּחַל | az huchal | then it was begun | chalal (hophal), passive: "it was begun," with no subject expressed (compare Gen 6:1, hechel, hiphil; 9:20; 10:8; 11:6). SP: החל, hechel, "he began" (hiphil, one letter); LXX: ēlpisen, "he hoped," as if from yachal. Added to Judging variants. KJV: "then began men to call," supplying "men." |
| לִקְרֹא בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה | liqro be-shem YHWH | to call on the name of YHWH | qara be-shem, "call on" or "call by" the name: Abram at his altars (12:8; 13:4; 21:33; 26:25, Isaac); YHWH proclaiming his own name (Ex 33:19; 34:5); Elijah against the Baal prophets (1 Kgs 18:24); Ps 116:4, 13, 17; Zeph 3:9; Joel 2:32. Not qara shem, the naming formula of 4:17, 25. The name YHWH is in the mouth of the woman in 4:1 and of Qayin in 4:13; the verse states the practice without naming who began it. KJV: "to call upon the name of the LORD." |
