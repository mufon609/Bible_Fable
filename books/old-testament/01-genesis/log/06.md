# Genesis 6 — verse log

One section per verse: bold translation line, pointed Hebrew, transliteration, then a table (Hebrew / Transliteration / Literal / Note). Notes state form and usage only. Rules in method/method.md.

Witnesses for this chapter: MT, SP and LXX throughout; Syriac and Vulgate cited where they differ. The one Dead Sea Scroll listed for the chapter is 6Q1 (6QpaleoGen), a paleo-Hebrew fragment given as Gen 6:13–21 in the published lists (Wikipedia's list of the scrolls and dssenglishbible.com, 2026-10-24), published by Baillet in DJD III (1962), pp. 105–106 (not consulted); its plate (PAM M42.576, Leon Levy image B-283864) was viewed on 2026-10-24 at the site's resolution and shows about ten lines of paleo-Hebrew, but the letters were not read; it is cited at 6:13 and 6:21 as the ends of its range, and its agreement with the MT rests on the published lists. The MT is the Leningrad text as archived in sources/wlc-uxlc (UXLC 2.6), accents stripped. The SP was compared verse by verse on 2026-10-24 against the archived consonantal text of MS Dublin Chester Beatty 751 (sources/sp-dt-ucph), not against the tanakh.info page used for ch. 3–5; von Gall and Tal–Florentin not consulted. The SP differs from the MT in ch. 6 in sense at 6:4 ("begot" for "bore"), 6:9 ("and whole"), 6:13 (the participle's suffix), 6:16 ("upon" for "to"), 6:19 ("the living-thing"; "and from all the flesh"; no "they shall be") and 6:20 ("and it shall be"; "all that creeps upon the ground to their kinds"), and in spelling only at 6:2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 15, 16, 17. The LXX is Swete's text (Codex Alexandrinus for Genesis, where Vaticanus is lacking) as archived in sources/lxx-swete-sollupulo and sources/lxx-swete; it differs in wording at 6:1 (verse division), 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17, 19, 20, 22. The Rahlfs–Hanhart text (2006) was opened at die-bibel.de in a browser on 2026-10-24: it begins 6:1 at the MT's 6:1 (5:32 separate), and reads hoi huioi tou theou, "the sons of God," at 6:2, en te genea autou, "in his generation," at 6:9, and enantion tou theou, "before God," at 6:11, where Swete's Alexandrinus text has "angels," "in his origin" and "the earth of God"; at every other point cited below the two editions agree. Göttingen not opened. The Vulgate is the Clementine text (sources/vulgate-clementine); the Syriac the ETCBC transcription of the Leiden text (sources/peshitta-etcbc), read in transliteration; Targum Onqelos from the Sefaria text (sources/targum-onqelos-sefaria), cited at 6:2 and 6:9 only.

The chapter has two parts by its names for God: YHWH is the subject in 6:1–8 (6:3, 5, 6, 7, 8) and Elohim in 6:9–22 (6:9, 11, 12, 13, 22), with ha-Elohim, with the article, in "sons of God" (6:2, 4), "with God" (6:9) and "before God" (6:11). The LXX writes "the Lord God" (kyrios ho theos) at 6:3, 5, 8, 12, 22 and "God" alone at 6:6, 7; the Vulgate writes Deus at 6:3, 5 where the Hebrew has YHWH.

*vayhi ki-hechel ha-adam larov al-penei ha-adamah u-vanot yulledu lahem*

## Genesis 6:3

**Translation: And YHWH said, "My spirit shall not abide in the human forever, in that also he is flesh; and his days shall be a hundred and twenty years."**

וַיֹּאמֶר יְהוָה לֹא־יָדוֹן רוּחִי בָאָדָם לְעֹלָם בְּשַׁגַּם הוּא בָשָׂר וְהָיוּ יָמָיו מֵאָה וְעֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה

*vayyomer YHWH lo-yadon ruchi va-adam le-olam be-shaggam hu vasar ve-hayu yamav me'ah ve-esrim shanah*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| לֹא־יָדוֹן רוּחִי בָאָדָם | lo-yadon ruchi va-adam | my spirit shall not abide in the human | ידון: the consonants are read by the vowels as yadon, a form found nowhere else. Two roots are near it: din, "judge, contend" (Gen 15:14; 30:6; 49:16; Deut 32:36; Ps 7:8; Eccl 6:10, "contend with him that is mightier"), whose imperfect is yadin; and dur/dun, "dwell," whose imperfect would be yadur. Every old translation read a verb of remaining: LXX ou me katameine, "shall not remain"; Vulgate non permanebit; Syriac la te'mar, "shall not dwell"; Onqelos la yitqayyam, "shall not endure." SP: ידון, the same letters. The rendering rests on the consonants and the four translations; the vowels alone fix no root. The log renders "abide" and flags the word; see Judging variants. ruach, "spirit," the word of 1:2 ("the spirit of God") and 3:8 ("the wind of the day"); with the suffix "my." ba-adam, "in the human," with the article. KJV: "My spirit shall not always strive with man." |
| לְעֹלָם | le-olam | forever | The phrase of 3:22 ("live forever"); SP לעולם, plene, same word. |
| בְּשַׁגַּם הוּא בָשָׂר | be-shaggam hu vasar | in that also he is flesh | בשגם: by the vowels three particles written as one word, be- "in," she- "that," gam "also": "in that also." The relative she- is otherwise the form of Judges 5:7; 6:17; Song 1:7; Eccl 1:7 and Psalms, not of Genesis; asher is the chapter's relative (6:2, 4, 7, 13, 15, 17, 21, 22). The consonants also permit a form of shagag, "err" (Lev 4:13; Num 15:28), with a suffix; no witness reads one. LXX: dia to einai autous sarkas, "because they are flesh," plural, with no "also"; Vulgate: quia caro est; Syriac: "because he is flesh"; SP: as MT. basar, "flesh," the word of 2:21, 23, 24; here and in 6:12, 13, 17, 19 the word for living bodies. KJV: "for that he also is flesh." |
| וְהָיוּ יָמָיו מֵאָה וְעֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה | ve-hayu yamav me'ah ve-esrim shanah | and his days shall be a hundred and twenty years | The Hebrew states the hundred before the twenty, the order of 5:5 (930) and the reverse of 5:3–31's other numbers (5:6, "five years and a hundred years"). "His days" with a singular suffix, the human as collective; the phrase yamav, "his days," is the one of 5:4, 5 ("the days of Adam"). 120 in all witnesses; Moses' age at death is 120 years (Deut 31:2; 34:7). The clause does not say of whom the days are counted or from when. LXX: "their days shall be a hundred and twenty years," plural. KJV: "yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years." |

## Genesis 6:22

**Translation: And Noach did according to all which God commanded him; so he did.**

וַיַּעַשׂ נֹחַ כְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר צִוָּה אֹתוֹ אֱלֹהִים כֵּן עָשָׂה

*vayya'as Noach ke-khol asher tzivvah oto Elohim ken asah*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיַּעַשׂ נֹחַ כְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר צִוָּה אֹתוֹ אֱלֹהִים | vayya'as Noach ke-khol asher tzivvah oto Elohim | and Noach did according to all which God commanded him | asah, "did, made," the verb of 6:6, 7, 14, 15, 16 (×2); the chapter's nine uses end with Noach as subject twice. tzavah (piel), "command," the verb of 2:16; 3:11, 17 ("the tree which I commanded you"); next 7:5, 9, 16. ke-khol asher… ken, "according to all which… so," the formula of obedience in Ex 39:32, 42, 43; 40:16 ("according to all which YHWH commanded Moses, so did the sons of Israel") and Num 1:54; 2:34; 5:4; 8:20; 9:5. Elohim, without the article, as 6:12, 13. The MT has a section break (ס) after the verse. LXX: kai epoiesen Noe panta hosa eneteilato auto kyrios ho theos, houtos epoiesen, with "the Lord God"; Vulgate: Fecit igitur Noe omnia quae praeceperat illi Deus, without the closing "so he did"; Syriac: "and Noach did all that God commanded him; so he did." KJV: "Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he." |
| כֵּן עָשָׂה | ken asah | so he did | The clause restates the verb: "did… so he did," the frame of the sentence. The same two words close 7:5 in the order asah… ke-khol asher tzivvahu YHWH. |
