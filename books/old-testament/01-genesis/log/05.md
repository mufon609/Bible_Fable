# Genesis 5 — verse log

One section per verse: bold translation line, pointed Hebrew, transliteration, then a table (Hebrew / Transliteration / Literal / Note). Notes state form and usage only. Rules in method/method.md.

Witnesses for this chapter: MT, SP and LXX throughout; Syriac and Vulgate cited where they differ. The one Dead Sea Scroll listed for the chapter is 4QGen-b, whose last fragment is given as 5:13 in two published lists and as 5:13–14 in a third (DJD XII, pp. 31–38, not consulted); it is cited at 5:13 only, and whether the number in that verse survives on the fragment is unchecked; plates not inspected. The SP consonants were compared verse by verse with the MT on 2026-10-24 (text as presented at tanakh.info; von Gall and Tal–Florentin not consulted); the SP differs from the MT in ch. 5 at 5:18, 19, 20, 25, 26, 27, 28, 30, 31 (numbers), 5:29 (number of a noun), 5:31 (number of a verb) and 5:32 (a name omitted). The LXX differs in the numbers at 5:3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13, 15, 16, 21, 22, 28, 30, 31, and in wording at 5:1, 3, 22, 24, 29, 32; its Greek was taken from the same online polyglot (the Greek there reads 187 and 782 at 5:25–26, where Rahlfs prints 167 and 802 from Codex Vaticanus; Göttingen not consulted). The Vulgate and Syriac follow the MT's numbers in every verse. The three sets of numbers are tabulated at 5:3 and judged in method/judging-variants.md.

The chapter is one formula said ten times. For each father: (a) "And X lived N years and begot Y"; (b) "And X lived after his begetting Y N years and begot sons and daughters"; (c) "And all the days of X were N years, and he died." The formula is given in full in the notes to 5:3–5 and 5:6–8; later sections note only what departs from it. Departures: Adam (5:3–5: "begot in his likeness," names the son, "the days of Adam were" for "Adam lived," "which he lived"), Chanokh (5:22–24: "walked about with God" for "lived," singular "was" in 5:23, no "and he died"), Lemekh (5:28–29: "begot a son" and names him with a reason), Noach (5:32: age given as "son of five hundred years," three sons named, no total and no death).

## Genesis 5:1

**Translation: This is the book of the begettings of Adam. In the day of God's creating human, in the likeness of God he made him.**

זֶה סֵפֶר תּוֹלְדֹת אָדָם בְּיוֹם בְּרֹא אֱלֹהִים אָדָם בִּדְמוּת אֱלֹהִים עָשָׂה אֹתוֹ

*zeh sefer toledot Adam be-yom bero Elohim adam bi-dmut Elohim asah oto*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| זֶה סֵפֶר תּוֹלְדֹת | zeh sefer toledot | this is the book of the begettings of | sefer, a written document: a letter (2 Sam 11:14), a deed (Jer 32:12), a scroll of the law (Deut 31:26), a register (Ex 32:32; Mal 3:16). First use. toledot, the heading word of 2:4 (see the note there); "book of the begettings" only here. Of the eleven toledot headings in Genesis this is the only one with sefer; the others have elleh, "these are" (2:4; 6:9; 10:1; 11:10, 27; 25:12, 19; 36:1, 9; 37:2). LXX: biblos geneseos anthropon, "book of the origin of humans," plural; SP: as MT. See Concordance. KJV: "the book of the generations of Adam." |
| אָדָם | Adam | Adam | אדם without the article, four times in 5:1–2 and as the subject of 5:3–5. In 5:1a it stands in construct after toledot, where the article cannot appear; the log renders it as the name because 5:3–5 continue with the same word as the subject of "lived," "begot," "died," the form first met at 4:25. SP: as MT. LXX: anthropon here, Adam in 5:1b. |
| בְּיוֹם בְּרֹא אֱלֹהִים אָדָם | be-yom bero Elohim adam | in the day of God's creating human | be-yom + infinitive construct, the construction of 2:4 (be-yom asot, "in the day of making") and 2:17 (be-yom akholkha). bara, the verb of 1:1, 21, 27; 2:3, 4; its next uses are 5:2 (twice) and 6:7. adam again without the article; here it is the object of "created" and is followed by "him" (oto) and in 5:2 by "them" (otam, shemam), the pattern of 1:27 ("created him… created them"). The log renders "human," the rendering of 1:26 (na'aseh adam), where the word is also without the article and takes plural pronouns. The letters do not distinguish this adam from the one before it; the pronouns do. LXX: epoiesen… ton Adam, "made Adam"; Vulgate: creavit… hominem, "created man"; Syriac: "created Adam." KJV: "God created man." |
| בִּדְמוּת אֱלֹהִים עָשָׂה אֹתוֹ | bi-dmut Elohim asah oto | in the likeness of God he made him | demut, the word of 1:26 (ki-dmutenu, "as our likeness"); there the preposition was ke-, "as," and the image took be-, "in"; here the likeness takes be-, "in." 25 uses; see Concordance. asah, "made," the verb of 1:26 and 1:31; 2:2–4; not bara, which the verse has just used. LXX: kat' eikona theou, "according to the image of God," the word of 1:27 for tzelem; SP: as MT. KJV: "in the likeness of God made he him." |

*zakhar u-neqevah bera'am vayvarekh otam vayyiqra et-shemam adam be-yom hibbare'am*

## Genesis 5:32

**Translation: And Noach was a son of five hundred years, and Noach begot Shem, Cham and Yefet.**

וַיְהִי־נֹחַ בֶּן־חֲמֵשׁ מֵאוֹת שָׁנָה וַיּוֹלֶד נֹחַ אֶת־שֵׁם אֶת־חָם וְאֶת־יָפֶת

*vayhi-Noach ben-chamesh me'ot shanah vayyoled Noach et-Shem et-Cham ve-et-Yafet*

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Literal | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיְהִי־נֹחַ בֶּן־חֲמֵשׁ מֵאוֹת שָׁנָה | vayhi-Noach ben-chamesh me'ot shanah | and Noach was a son of five hundred years | ben + a number of years, "son of N years": the Hebrew statement of age (7:6; 11:10; 12:4; 16:16; 17:1, 24–25; 21:5). The first use of the idiom; the formula's "X lived N years" is not used for Noach. 500 in all witnesses. |
| וַיּוֹלֶד נֹחַ אֶת־שֵׁם אֶת־חָם וְאֶת־יָפֶת | vayyoled Noach et-Shem et-Cham ve-et-Yafet | and Noach begot Shem, Cham and Yefet | Three sons in one line, each with et; no "sons and daughters," no total, no death: the chapter ends inside Noach's line, which 9:28–29 completes in the formula's words ("and all the days of Noach were… and he died"). shem is the common noun "name" of 5:2–3, 29; cham, "hot" (Josh 9:12); yefet, pausal Yafet, is glossed in 9:27 by yaft, "may he enlarge." SP omits the second Noach (ויולד את שם); LXX adds treis huious, "three sons"; Vulgate: as MT in sense. KJV: "Shem, Ham, and Japheth." |
