# Concordance — Hebrew

Every term the Findings lean on, traced across the Hebrew Bible. One rendering per term, fixed here and used wherever the word occurs. Each entry gives the total, the distribution by sense with counts, the occurrences that do not fit the pattern the log draws from it, and the weight the parallel can carry. *Status* says whether the verse list is complete or representative; representative lists are to be checked against a full concordance before the pattern is cited as settled.

**Reading the table.** A parallel's weight is its specificity: *nefesh chayyah* and *raqia* carry a pattern on their own; *mo'ed* and the *avad*/*shamar* pair carry it only as the most frequent of several senses, and the Findings should say so.
