# Method

Migrated 2026-10-24 from the Genesis Literal Translation Log (started 2026-10-09). Project-wide; applies to every book.

Every rendering is built from the Hebrew consonantal text, checked against the oldest witnesses, and flagged wherever a reading depends on medieval vowel points.

| Witness | Date | What it gives us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dead Sea Scrolls: 4QGen-b (1:1–27; 2:14–19), 4QGen-g (1:1–11, 13–22; 2:6–7), 4QGen-d (1:18–27), 4QGen-h1 (1:8–10), 4QGen-h2 (2:17–18), 4QGen-k (1:9, 14–16, 27–28; 2:1–3; 3:1–2), 1QGen (1:18–21; 3:11–14) | \~1st c. BCE | Oldest Hebrew consonants. Nearly all of ch. 1 is attested; of ch. 2 only 2:1–3, 6–7 and 14–19. Where attested they match the Masoretic consonants. A verse not in this list has no DSS witness, and the log does not cite one. |
| Septuagint (LXX) | \~3rd c. BCE | Oldest translation (Greek); independent check on how the Hebrew was read |
| Samaritan Pentateuch | \~2nd c. BCE tradition | Second Hebrew consonantal tradition |
| Masoretic Text (Aleppo \~930 CE, Leningrad 1008 CE) | 10th–11th c. CE | Vowel points and accents; the base of every modern Bible |

Rules applied: one English word per Hebrew word where English allows; verb forms (perfect, sequential, jussive, participle) kept distinct; articles and plurals rendered exactly as the Hebrew has them; a word with a range of meaning is settled by how Genesis and the immediate verse use it, never by doctrine.

Parallels and conclusions: a note records where else the Bible uses the same word or phrase and what it means there; it does not say what the parallel means for the verse. Conclusions drawn from parallels go on the Findings tab, stated as what the vocabulary shares, not as what the text intends. Extra-biblical data (cognate languages, Near Eastern practice) enters a note only to fix a word's meaning (Akkadian *edû* for 2:6, *Idiglat*, *Purattu*), never to supply a picture the Hebrew does not state.

Weight of a parallel: a parallel is recorded with its full distribution, the occurrences that fit and the ones that do not, and its weight is stated as a count. A term the Findings lean on gets an entry on the Concordance tab before the pattern is cited as settled.

Products and evidence: the Readable translation is the product for readers; the Findings are the product of the study. The verse tables, variant judgments, belief table and Concordance are their evidence. A term the Findings lean on is traced on the Concordance tab before the pattern is cited as settled.

Transliteration: *ch* for ח, *kh* for soft כ, *tz* for צ, *q* for ק, *sh* for שׁ, *s* for שׂ and ס; *'* for ע and א within a word, unmarked at the start of a word (so \*esev\*, \*etz\*, \*al-penei\*, but \*ma'or\*, \*vayya'as\*); doubled consonants for dagesh forte (*chayyah*, *yabbashah*); construct forms as pronounced (*chayyat*). The existing tables follow this; new entries do too.
