# Septuagint, Swete's edition (Sollupulo proofread transcription), Genesis

**What it is.** *The Old Testament in Greek According to the Septuagint*, edited by Henry Barclay Swete, vol. I (Genesis to IV Kings), 4th edition, Cambridge University Press, 1909 (1925 reprint), body text only, transcribed and proofread character for character against the Internet Archive scan (https://archive.org/details/theoldtestamenti00unknuoft) by the GitHub user Sollupulo. Marginal readings and the apparatus are not reproduced. A second, independently made transcription of the same edition is in `../lxx-swete/`. Not Rahlfs.
